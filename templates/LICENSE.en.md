## Article 1: Scope and Factual Premise

This declaration applies to a repository or content set (the "Subject Content") for which the following factual premises hold:

1. The Subject Content was generated by a large language model (LLM) or other automated processing system.
2. The maintainer (the person applying this declaration) only provided a goal, purpose, requirements, or instructions for generating the Subject Content.
3. The maintainer did not creatively write, select, arrange, edit, supervise, or author the concrete expression of any source code, configuration files, workflows, templates, prompts, documents, articles, summaries, headings, classifications, explanatory text, or other specific outputs.

If the above factual premises do not hold, this declaration may not function as intended.
